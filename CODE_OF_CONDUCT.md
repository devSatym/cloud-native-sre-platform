# Code of Conduct

Cloud-Native Reliability Engineering Platform is a learning and portfolio project.
Everyone participating in its discussions or contributions is expected to be respectful,
constructive, and professional.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Welcome questions from people with different levels of experience.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, unwanted attention, or publishing private information.
- Handle operational evidence and credentials responsibly.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security/advisory channel or
to the current repository maintainer. Reports should be handled confidentially and
without retaliation.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, maintainers may request a correction, issue a warning, or remove
access to the project. This document is adapted from the principles of the Contributor
Covenant.
